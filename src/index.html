Flights and space

Flights shows each received aircraft individually on the detailed 2D map, with no numbered groups. Space shows a rotating Earth, calculated satellite positions and the selected orbital trajectory. Drag to move, scroll to zoom, select an object for details.

Freshness and quotas

Automatic refresh spreads the remaining public traffic budget over the rest of the UTC day and preserves a reserve. Budget and the latest dated report survive restart. Old positions are dimmed and labelled; movement is projected for at most 15 seconds. No worldwide ten-second live service or exhaustive coverage is promised.

Satellites and fallback coverage

Positions are calculated locally with SGP4 every five seconds, not measured telemetry. CelesTrak CSV elements are checked no more than once per two hours. A dated starter catalogue of 100 popular objects from TLE API is included for first launch and outages. This fallback is a limited selection, not the full CelesTrak active catalogue. Element epoch and age appear in each card. Requests pause after provider errors.

Maps

OpenStreetMap provides cities and streets. Sentinel-2 2025 offers global imagery at about 10 m per pixel for noncommercial use. Switzerland HD covers Switzerland only. Detail and availability depend on providers. NASA overview maps work offline.

Personal observation only. Never use Airview for navigation, aircraft separation or operational air traffic control.

Shortcuts: / search, Space pause, R recenter, F full screen, H help, +/− zoom, Escape clear selection. Use arrow keys to rotate the focused globe.

Traffic: OpenSky / ADS-B.lol. Enrichment: ADSBdb. Orbits: CelesTrak / TLE API / satellite.js. Airports: OurAirports. Map attribution remains on screen.
